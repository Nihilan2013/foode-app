Tips

These cooking tips are designed to help you get better results across every recipe.

Quick Tips

  • Use fresh herbs and spices for authentic flavor.
  • Traditional recipes can have regional variations.
  • Serve with local breads, salads or sauces for better taste.
  • Adjust spice level according to your preference.

Common Tips for All Recipes

  1. Wash all vegetables, meat and rice properly before cooking.
  2. Use fresh ingredients for better taste and smell.
  3. Prepare and measure ingredients before starting.
  4. Marinate meat for extra flavor and softness.
  5. Cook on medium flame to avoid burning.
  6. Stir food regularly while cooking.
  7. Add spices slowly and taste while cooking.
  8. Use enough water while boiling rice or pasta.
  9. Keep kitchen tools and hands clean.
  10. Garnish with herbs, nuts or sauces for better presentation.
  11. Serve food hot for best taste.
  12. Store leftover food in refrigerator properly.
  13. Follow cooking time carefully.
  14. Use oil or butter in correct quantity.
  15. Practice regularly to improve cooking skills.
